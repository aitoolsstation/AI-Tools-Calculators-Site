❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How to calculate female body fat percentage accurately?
Our female body fat calculator uses the US Navy method with waist, neck, and hip measurements. It's updated for current health standards and provides accurate results. Simply select "Female" and enter your measurements.
What are the GST rates in India?
GST rates: 0% (essential food), 5% (restaurant), 12% (clothes, processed food), 18% (electronics, services), 28% (luxury, automobiles). Hotel tariffs: 0% (<₹1000), 12% (₹1000-2499), 18% (₹2500+).
Is the body fat calculator free?
Yes, all calculators including the female body fat calculator are completely free. No registration required. We support the site through ads and donations.
